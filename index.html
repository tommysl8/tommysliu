Projects

Aug 2024 – May 2025 · Published in EDN

Low-Cost High-Precision Digital Oscilloscope Using Analog/Digital Signal Processing

Designed and built a low cost digital oscilloscope aimed at making electronics more accessible to high school students. Improved the base model oscilloscope's effective resolution through analog and digital signal processing techniques, achieving the following specifications:

8 to >10 bitsENOB, DC to 20kHz
<$50final BOM cost

This project was featured as a two-part design idea article in Electronic Design News (EDN) and won awards from MIT and IEEE.

EDN, Part 1 EDN, Part 2 Project notebook

Jun 2025 – Jun 2026 · UC Merced PANSLAB · Published in EDN

Batteryless Mobile PM2.5 Air Quality Monitoring System

Designed the sensing and power-generation circuits for a batteryless, mobile particulate matter monitor, built for a state-sponsored effort to map community air quality across California's Central Valley. The system runs on harvested energy instead of batteries, so units can be deployed and left in place without service visits.

Violin

When I say I love violin, I mean it. In my opinion, classical music is the best music.

I have been studying violin with Dr. Arthur Mikhailov since the age of five (12 years). Dr. Mikhailov graduated from the Moscow State Conservatory, previously directed the Moscow Conservatory Chamber Orchestra, and is currently the Head of the String Department at the Pacific Music Academy (PMA) in California.

I currently serve as the Principal First Violin and lead the string section of the PMA Youth Chamber Orchestra.
